Under **People**, invite by email. An invitation can already carry a **role**.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing and roles. |
| Admin | Manage agents, connections and people. |
| Member | Talk to agents and use what was allowed. |

You can create your own roles with specific permissions. Nobody can change or grant a role above their own.

## Channels

A **channel** gathers people and agents around a subject. Everyone in the channel sees the conversation; use `@` to call an agent. Channels can be **private**, visible only to the people chosen by their creator.

A person's own conversations with an agent stay theirs.
