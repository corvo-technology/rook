## Sign up

1. Open Rook Cloud and enter your email. You get a one-time code; there is no password to remember.
2. Name your space (usually the company name). A **space** holds the team's people, agents, channels, connections and memory.
3. Follow the onboarding.

## Onboarding

Onboarding is a short rail led by the **Chief**, the agent that coordinates the others. It asks what your company does, suggests a starting team from a template and shows a live preview of what it will create. You adjust and confirm; nothing is created without your yes.

You can skip it and come back: everything it does is also available in the regular screens.

## The Home

After onboarding you land on the **Home**: what is happening right now.

- agents and what each one is doing;
- approvals waiting for you;
- routines that ran and what they delivered;
- a suggested next step, while something is still unconfigured.

The sidebar opens the rest: Home, Approvals, Conversations, Tasks, Team, Channels, Agents and People.
