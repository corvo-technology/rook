A **routine** is an instruction with a schedule. Ask in chat or create it on the agent's page:

> Every Monday at 8am, summarize last week's sales and post it in #sales.

When the time comes the agent runs with the same connections, memory and wiki as in a normal conversation and delivers the result in the channel, with the routine's name on the message.

- A routine with nothing to say may stay silent; say so in the instructions ("only tell me if something is new").
- [Approvals](/en/start/approvals/) apply too: a routine that needs to change something waits for your yes.
- The agent's [budget](/en/agents/models/#budgets) also covers its routines.
